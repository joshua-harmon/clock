
8th UNDERWORLD - THE LOCAL GROUP
                          
1.392 million light years to the center of mass.
820 million years time frame.
            
  The Local Group is the home cluster of galaxies, spanning about 5-6 million light years 
across.  It has about 21 known members.  The constellations Ursa Minor and Draco are actually 
galaxies in the Local Group.
  Three of the members of the Local Group are spiral galaxies.  The largest spiral galaxy in the 
Local Group is the Andromeda Galaxy (M31).  It has a diameter of about 130 thousand light years. 
The third and smallest spiral galaxy, M32, is relatively near M31.
  Andromeda Galaxy is about 2 million light years from "our own" spiral galaxy, and is basically
the closest other spiral galaxy to us. We yet can measure its movement as it moves toward us, 
300km/s on one end, and 600km/s (approx 3.5 million miles per hour) at the other end.
                  

9th UNDERWORLD - THE UNIVERSE

27.84 million light years to the center of mass.
16 billion 400 million yrs time frame. 

  The universe is an uncountable collection of galactic clusters.  In the north sky alone,
2712+ clusters have so far been catalogued.  The closest to the Local Group is the Virgo
Galactic Cluster, about 4 million light years across, and at about 40 million light years away.
Clusters may typically range to about 15 million light years across.
