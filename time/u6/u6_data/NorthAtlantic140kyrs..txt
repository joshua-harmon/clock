DESCRIPTION: 
North-Atlantic paleotemperature data set from Fig. 2 of Bard (2002),
with age model updated as in Bard et al. (2004).
These data correspond to the temperature record for the Iberian Margin.
Site location (stack of deep-sea cores SU8118 and MD952042): 37°45'N, 10°10'W

Sea-surface temperatures were measured with the alkenone method 
(calibration of Prahl et al. 1988).  The time scale is compatible with the 
new GRIP chronology of Johnsen et al. (2001).

Geochemical and geochronological data, together with some interpretations, 
can be found in the following papers:
Bard et al. (1987)
Bard et al. (2000)
Pailler & Bard (2002)
Bard et al. (2004)
